Privacy Policy

Last updated: date

This policy explains what data Clear Host collects when you play on our public Minecraft servers or visit this website, and what we do with it. Items highlighted in yellow are placeholders to fill in before you publish.

Who we are

Clear Host runs public Minecraft servers. Contact us at you@example.com with any privacy question.

What we collect on our servers

  • Minecraft username and UUID. Needed to identify you in game.
  • IP address. Needed to connect you, enforce bans and block abuse.
  • Chat and command logs. Kept for moderation.
  • Gameplay data. Things like your inventory, builds, playtime, permissions and punishments, stored by the server and its plugins.
  • Anything else you collect, such as Discord IDs if you link accounts, or website or store data.

How we use it

  • To run the server and save your progress.
  • To moderate chat, enforce rules and handle bans and reports.
  • To protect the server from abuse, cheating and attacks.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Minecraft accounts are verified by Mojang and Microsoft, which have their own privacy policies. Other players can see your username, chat messages and in-game activity. We may share data with our hosting provider (provider name) or if the law requires it. List any plugins or third-party services that receive data.

How long we keep it

Chat and command logs are kept for 30 days. Gameplay data is kept while your account is active on the server. Ban records are kept for as long as the ban applies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you or to delete it, except where we need to keep it to enforce a ban or comply with the law. Email you@example.com with your Minecraft username.

Children

Our servers are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies or analytics on it. GitHub may log visitor data under its own privacy statement.

Security

We restrict access to server data to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
