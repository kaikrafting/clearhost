Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Clear Host collects when you play on our public Minecraft servers, join our Discord server, or visit this website. It also explains why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. Items highlighted in yellow are placeholders to fill in before you publish.

In short: we collect only what we need to run and moderate our servers, we use the third-party services listed below to do that, and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

Clear Host is the operator of the public Minecraft servers and related Discord community described on this site. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the "controller" of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: support@clearhost.co

2. What we collect

When you play on our Minecraft servers

  • Minecraft username and UUID. Used to identify you in game and to link your data to your account.
  • IP address. Used to connect you, enforce bans, detect ban evasion and block attacks and abuse.
  • Connection and session data. When you log in and out, and how long you play.
  • Chat and command logs. What you type in public and private chat and which commands you run. Used for moderation and investigating reports.
  • In-game activity logs. Block placements and removals, container (chest) interactions, item transactions, kills and similar actions, with your username, location and time. Used to investigate and repair griefing and theft.
  • Gameplay and account data. Inventory, progress, permissions, rank, playtime, warnings, mutes, kicks and bans, stored by the server and its plugins.

When you use our Discord server

  • Discord user ID and username, and your roles on our server.
  • Messages and content you post in our Discord server, including messages relayed to or from Minecraft chat.
  • Account link data. If you link your Minecraft and Discord accounts, we store the connection between your Minecraft UUID and your Discord user ID.

When you visit this website

We do not run analytics or set cookies on this site. Our hosting provider for the site, GitHub, may collect technical data such as your IP address and browser details in its server logs (see section 4).

3. Why we use your data

  • To provide the servers and save your progress (performance of the service you asked to use).
  • To moderate chat, enforce our rules, and handle reports and appeals (our legitimate interest in running a safe community).
  • To protect the servers, our providers and other players from cheating, abuse and denial-of-service attacks (legitimate interest and security).
  • To link your Minecraft and Discord accounts and relay chat between them, if you choose to use these features (your consent).
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the legal bases we rely on are: performance of a contract, legitimate interests, consent, and legal obligation, as noted above. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us or unlinking your account.

4. Third-party services we use

We use the following services to run our servers. They process data on our behalf or according to their own policies. We choose providers carefully, but we do not control their systems.

Hosting providers: Pebble Host and Winter Node

Our Minecraft servers run on infrastructure provided by Pebble Host and Winter Node. Server files, world data, plugin databases and logs are stored on their systems, and they process your IP address when you connect. Servers may be located in the United States, so your data may be transferred outside your own country.

Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare for DNS and for protection against attacks and abusive traffic. Cloudflare may process your IP address and connection metadata when you connect to our domain. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

CoreProtect

CoreProtect is a server plugin that logs in-game activity so staff can inspect and roll back griefing. It stores block changes, container interactions, kills, chat, commands and login sessions, linked to your username or UUID, in a database on our servers. Data collected by CoreProtect is not sent to a third party by us.

DiscordSRV

DiscordSRV is a server plugin that connects our Minecraft servers to our Discord server. It relays chat, join and leave messages and other events, and lets players link their accounts. It stores account links (Minecraft UUID and Discord user ID) on our servers and sends the relayed content to Discord.

Discord

We run a community on Discord. Discord collects and processes your data under its own terms. See the Discord Privacy Policy. You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement to use it.

Mojang and Microsoft

Minecraft accounts are authenticated by Mojang and Microsoft. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We are not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft.

GitHub

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

5. We do not sell your data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We only disclose it in the following cases:

  • To the service providers listed in section 4, so that they can provide their services to us.
  • To other players, for the information you make public by playing or chatting (your username, chat messages and in-game actions are visible to others).
  • Where required by law, court order or a valid legal request, or to protect the safety and rights of players, staff or others.
  • In connection with a sale or transfer of the servers, in which case we will tell you and the new owner will be bound by this policy.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Chat and command logs: 180 days
  • CoreProtect activity logs: 90 days, or your configured purge period
  • IP address logs: 90 days
  • Gameplay and account data: for as long as your account is active on the server, then 12 months after your last login
  • Ban and punishment records: for as long as the ban or punishment applies, plus 180 day period to prevent evasion
  • Discord account links: until you unlink or ask us to remove them
  • Backups: kept for a 30 day period and then overwritten

Discord and our hosting providers keep data according to their own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of these rights:

  • To know what personal data we hold about you and to get a copy.
  • To correct data that is wrong.
  • To ask us to delete your data.
  • To restrict or object to how we use it.
  • To receive your data in a portable format.
  • To withdraw consent where we rely on it.
  • To not be discriminated against for using these rights.

To use any of these, email support@clearhost.co with your Minecraft username (and Discord username if relevant). We may ask you to prove the account is yours before acting on a request, and will respond within 30 days. We may refuse or limit a request where we must keep data to enforce a ban, protect the community, or meet a legal obligation, and we will explain why.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you are a California resident, you have the rights described in the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined there. We do not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track visitors across sites.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

Our servers and Discord community are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Discord and Minecraft also set their own age requirements. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. In some countries the age of digital consent is higher than 13, and we will honor local rules where they apply.

10. Security

We limit access to server data, logs and databases to staff who need it, and we use the protections offered by our providers. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and any required authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our servers or services change. We will change the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will also post a notice in our Discord server.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: support@clearhost.co
